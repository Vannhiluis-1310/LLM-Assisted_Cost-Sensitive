## Notebook 04 Scope

Notebook 04 implements:

- Level 3: one global cost-sensitive threshold tuned on validation only.
- Level 4: separate validation-tuned thresholds per `TransactionAmt` bin.
- Leakage guard: amount-bin boundaries are derived from train rows if available in the score export, otherwise validation amounts without using validation labels; test is report-only.
- Closed-form guard: the notebook explicitly states that the pure expected-cost threshold is not amount-dynamic because `TransactionAmt` cancels from both sides.
- Output tables for dynamic metrics, global thresholds, amount-bin thresholds, amount-bin boundaries, and five-level comparison.
- Output figures for amount-bin distribution, per-bin thresholds, total cost, cost saving, and recall/precision.
