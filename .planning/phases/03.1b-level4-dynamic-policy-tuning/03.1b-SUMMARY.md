# Phase 3.1b Summary: Level 4 Dynamic Policy Tuning

## What Changed

Phase 3.1b adds a new notebook-only tuning layer for the Phase 3.1 Level 4 dynamic policy. It does not retrain LightGBM/XGBoost, does not modify Q-bandit ablations, and does not implement Level 5.
